# Children's Bookstore Website — Proposal

Nov 19, 2026 · Prepared by Shailendra Maharjan

## Overview

We will build an online children's bookstore where families anywhere in Nepal can browse books and pay with eSewa, Khalti or cash on delivery. It will run on WordPress + WooCommerce, so your team can add books, set prices and handle orders yourselves, without needing a developer for day-to-day work.

## What customers can do

Parents and gift buyers can find the right book for a child in a few clicks, then order it from their phone.

- **Browse by age group:** 0–3, 4–7, 8–12 years
- **Browse by language:** Nepali, English
- **Browse by type:** picture books, stories, activity books, educational
- **Search** by title, author or keyword
- **Gift for a child:** pick an age and budget and see suitable books
- **Book pages** with cover photo, price, age range, language, page count and a short description
- **Cart and checkout** that work well on mobile, with no account required (guest checkout)
- **Order confirmation** by email, with an order number they can quote by phone, and an order status they can check

## Payments and delivery

Customers can pay three ways, and books can be delivered anywhere in Nepal.

| Method | How it works | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eSewa | Customer is sent to eSewa to pay, then returns to the site; the order is marked paid automatically | An eSewa merchant account |
| Khalti | Same flow through Khalti (official WooCommerce plugin) | A Khalti merchant account |
| Cash on delivery | Customer pays the courier on delivery; you mark the order paid | Agreement with your courier on cash collection |

**Delivery charges** will be set by zone, for example one rate inside Kathmandu Valley and another outside it. A free-delivery threshold (e.g. orders of NPR 2,000 or more) can be added. You can change these rates yourselves at any time.

**Couriers:** orders can be handed to a delivery partner such as Pathao, Upaya or Nepal Can Move; we will set up whichever you already use.

## What your team manages

Everything day-to-day is done from a simple admin panel in the browser, with no coding.

- **Books:** add a new book with cover photo, price, age group, language and type; edit or hide it later
- **Prices and sales:** change prices, set a sale price with start and end dates
- **Stock:** set quantities; a book shows "out of stock" automatically when it runs out, and you get a low-stock email
- **Orders:** see new orders, mark them packed, shipped and delivered, and confirm cash-on-delivery payments
- **Discount codes:** e.g. 10% off for Dashain or for a school
- **Homepage:** choose featured books, new arrivals and banners

We will provide a short training session and a step-by-step guide (with screenshots) for these tasks at handover.

## Recommended platform: WordPress + WooCommerce

WooCommerce is the best fit because it supports eSewa, Khalti and cash on delivery, and gives your team an easy admin panel.

| Option | Strengths | Drawbacks | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **WordPress + WooCommerce** | Easy admin panel; Khalti and eSewa plugins available; cash on delivery and delivery zones built in; low hosting cost; many developers in Nepal know it | Needs regular updates; must be kept lean to stay fast | **Recommended** |
| Custom-built site (e.g. Next.js + Medusa) | Full control over design and speed | Much longer and costlier to build; every change needs a developer; less familiar admin | Only if needs outgrow WooCommerce |
| Shopify | Polished, fully hosted | No eSewa or Khalti support; monthly fee in US dollars | Not suitable |

The site will use a custom, child-friendly design (bright colours, large book covers, clear age labels) rather than a generic template look, and will be built mobile-first, since most customers will order from their phones.

## Running costs and upkeep

Yearly running costs are low. Figures below are approximate and will be confirmed with quotes.

| Item | Approx. cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web hosting | NPR 3,000–8,000 / year | Shared WordPress hosting; can upgrade as orders grow |
| Domain (.com or .com.np) | NPR 1,500–2,000 / year for .com | .com.np is free for Nepali businesses (registration required) |
| SSL certificate (padlock) | Free | Included with most hosting |
| eSewa / Khalti fees | Per-transaction fee | Set by each provider in your merchant agreement |
| Maintenance (optional) | To be agreed | Monthly updates, backups and security checks |

WordPress and its plugins need updates every month or so to stay secure. We can handle this under a maintenance plan, or train your team to do it.
